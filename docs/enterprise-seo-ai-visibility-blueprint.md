# MedPobeda Group MCHJ Enterprise SEO and AI Visibility Blueprint

## 1. Executive Summary

MedPobeda Group can become discoverable quickly, but only if the site is treated as a YMYL healthcare property from day one:

- precise medical claims
- strong business identity
- specific service and location pages
- technical crawlability
- entity-rich structured data
- consistent multilingual architecture
- visible trust signals around KIMS Hospitals India, governance, contact, and patient process

The current repo already has a usable baseline:

- localized routes
- `robots.ts`
- `sitemap.ts`
- canonical metadata
- hreflang metadata
- core schema helpers

The current gaps are commercial and topical, not just technical:

- no strong KIMS partnership authority layer
- not enough treatment-specific landing pages
- not enough Central Asia country-intent pages
- organization schema is too generic for a medical tourism business
- no explicit AI-search crawler policy
- no deep E-E-A-T framework for a healthcare YMYL site

## 2. Critical Corrections Before Publishing SEO Claims

1. Keep the KIMS relationship wording legally precise.
Suggested wording:
`MedPobeda Group MCHJ is positioned as the exclusive strategic healthcare partner in Uzbekistan for KIMS Hospitals India.`

2. Do not publish the "24 hospitals" count unless your signed partner materials still require it.
As of May 18, 2026, the KIMS Hospitals official homepage describes a network of `27 hospitals` across India. For public SEO copy, the safer phrasing is:
`KIMS Hospitals is one of India's leading multi-specialty healthcare groups.`

3. Avoid "best", "guaranteed", "top doctor", "guaranteed visa", or "guaranteed treatment outcome" claims.

4. Treat the site as a healthcare YMYL property.
That means:
- real organization details
- real contact data
- real medical process explanations
- real authorship and editorial review
- no fake testimonials or numbers

## 12. Core Web Vitals Strategy

- use `next/image` for all non-critical and critical imagery
- serve AVIF/WebP where possible
- keep hero image below 180 KB compressed
- preload the primary hero image only
- lazy-load gallery and secondary images
- keep CLS stable with fixed aspect ratios
- reduce JS hydration in non-essential decorative sections
- cache static assets aggressively at CDN level
- keep LCP target under `2.5s`
- keep INP under `200ms`
- keep CLS under `0.1`

### ChatGPT Search

### Claude

- allow `ClaudeBot` if you want web discoverability

### Perplexity

- allow `PerplexityBot`
- optionally allow Perplexity IP ranges in WAF if bot access is filtered

### Gemini and Google AI

- there is no separate Gemini indexing stack for your site
- indexed, crawlable, people-first pages eligible for Google Search are the foundation for AI Overviews and AI Mode visibility
